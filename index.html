Step 4: Legal Setup

Get your business registered in South Africa

Sole Proprietor (Easiest)

Cost: R0

Best for: Solo freelancers, small services, informal traders

Status: Simple. Just you. No official CIPC registration needed to start.

Partnership Joint Venture

Cost: R0 - R500 (For a legal agreement)

Best for: 2 or more partners running an informal business together

Status: Requires a solid written agreement outlining profit shares.

Private Company (Pty Ltd)

Cost: R125 - R175

Best for: Growing businesses wanting to look professional or apply for funding

Status: Registered formally via CIPC (BizPortal).
